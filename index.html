About me

Political scientist exploring how populism reshapes democratic legitimacy, political communication and Africa–Europe relations.

I am an early-career researcher in law and political sciences. I completed my doctoral defence at the University of Szeged in June 2026, with a dissertation on populism in Africa as a political constant, a leadership legitimator and a democratic determinant.

My work asks what populist movements mean for institutional trust, electoral politics and political culture in Sub-Saharan Africa, and how those dynamics compare with Europe. I mostly use comparative qualitative methods and discourse analysis, and I write for academic, policy and public audiences.

I am a non-residential fellow at the European Centre for Populism Studies, where I was Deputy Managing Editor until early 2026. More on my work is in the publications, research and projects sections, I also bring close to a decade of editorial experience and am open to editorial work. You are welcome to get in touch.

    About

    I am an early-career political scientist whose research examines populism, democratic legitimacy and political communication in Sub-Saharan Africa and in comparative perspective.

    I completed a PhD in Law and Political Sciences at the University of Szeged, supervised by Associate Professor Norbert Merkovity. My dissertation, Populism in Africa: Political Constant, Leadership Legitimator and Democratic Determinant, was defended in June 2026.

    I hold an MA in International Relations from the University of Pécs and Honours and BA degrees in Politics, International Relations and Political Sciences from the University of Johannesburg. I was a Stipendium Hungaricum scholar (2019 and 2021).

    Beyond the academy, I have close to a decade of editorial, policy and media experience. I was Deputy Managing Editor at the European Centre for Populism Studies, where he conceived the centre’s first panel on African democracy and populism, and earlier led the Africa Desk at The Joburg Post.

    Editorial work

    Alongside research, I bring close to a decade of editorial experience. At the European Centre for Populism Studies he coordinated concurrent publication pipelines spanning journal articles, policy reports, commissioned research and edited volumes, and curated a quarterly newsletter reaching partners in more than 15 countries. I previously ran the Africa Desk at The Joburg Post as its sole editor and supported the editorial process and production of The Thinker at Vusizwe Media. I also review manuscripts for several journals.

    Available for editorial work. I am open to editing and proofreading of manuscripts and policy briefs, managing publication pipelines, and newsletter and content management. Get in touch to discuss a project.
